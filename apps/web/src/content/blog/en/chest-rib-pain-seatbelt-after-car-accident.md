The day after the crash, your chest hurts when you breathe deeply, cough or roll over in bed. There is a purple stripe running from your shoulder to your hip: the seatbelt mark.

First, the important part: **that seatbelt probably saved you from something much worse.** But stopping you with the full force of the impact leaves a mark, and it is worth knowing when that mark is just a bruise and when it is something more.

## What the seatbelt can injure

The shoulder strap crosses the chest and the lap strap crosses the hips. In a sudden stop, all of your body's force is concentrated on those two lines. According to the medical literature, the belt can cause anything from chest wall bruising to rib or sternum fractures and, less often, bruising of the lung or heart.

A seatbelt mark on the chest does not always mean a serious internal injury, but it is a reason to get checked. And if the mark is across your belly, the evaluation needs to be more careful, because the organs in the abdomen can be injured.

The same belt also loads the shoulder and collarbone: see [shoulder pain after a car accident](/en/blog/shoulder-pain-after-car-accident).

## How to look after yourself while it heals

If you have been checked and there are no red flags, the UK's National Health Service (NHS) recommends:

<div class="pa-check">
  <h4>Caring for bruised ribs</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Take pain relief as your doctor directs, so you can breathe and move.</li>
    <li>Apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for the first few days.</li>
    <li>Breathe normally and take slow, deep breaths several times a day.</li>
    <li>When you cough, hold a pillow against your chest. Do not hold the cough in.</li>
    <li>Walk around and move your shoulders. Do not stay still for long.</li>
    <li>For the first few nights, sleep more upright.</li>
    <li>Avoid lifting, straining and smoking.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

**Why so much emphasis on deep breathing?** Because when it hurts, people take shallow breaths and stop coughing, and mucus builds up. That can turn into a chest infection. Get care soon if you cough up yellow or green mucus, have a fever or chills, or the pain has not improved after a few weeks.

## The mark is also evidence

Photograph the seatbelt bruise the same day and the days after: it changes color and fades. It is visible proof of the force of the impact and that you were wearing your belt. Many symptoms show up late, so tell the examiner everything. Read more in [delayed symptoms after a car accident](/en/blog/delayed-symptoms-after-car-accident).

## The short version

Chest pain after a crash is usually a seatbelt bruise or an injured rib, and most heal in 2 to 6 weeks. But shortness of breath, coughing up blood, pressure-like pain or belly pain are emergencies. Get checked, breathe deeply even when it hurts, and keep wearing your seatbelt every time.
